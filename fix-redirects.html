Fix Redirect Issues

Clear Your Browser Cache

If you're seeing a "too many redirects" error, the first step is to clear your browser cache:

On Chrome:

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Command+Shift+Delete (Mac)
  2. Select "Cached images and files"
  3. Click "Clear data"

On Firefox:

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Command+Shift+Delete (Mac)
  2. Select "Cache"
  3. Click "Clear Now"

On Safari:

  1. Go to Safari menu > Preferences > Advanced
  2. Check "Show Develop menu in menu bar"
  3. Go to Develop > Empty Caches

Delete Cookies for advantis.care

Sometimes cookies can cause redirect loops:

On Chrome:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  2. Click "See all cookies and site data"
  3. Search for "advantis.care"
  4. Click the trash icon to delete these cookies

On Firefox:

  1. Go to Options > Privacy & Security
  2. Under Cookies and Site Data, click "Manage Data"
  3. Search for "advantis.care"
  4. Click "Remove Selected"

Try Incognito/Private Mode

Opening the site in incognito or private browsing mode can help bypass caching issues:

  • Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+N (Windows) or Command+Shift+N (Mac)
  • Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows) or Command+Shift+P (Mac)
  • Safari: Command+Shift+N
